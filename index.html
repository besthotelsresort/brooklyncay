About Aurora Stays

A curator's eye, not an endless list

Aurora Stays began in Halifax in 2016 as a private list of the hotels our founding editor kept recommending to friends. It grew into a small, independent review house — and a deliberately short one.

We are not a booking engine and we are not owned by a hotel group. Our writers pay their own way, stay anonymously where they can, and describe what really happens between check-in and check-out — the gracious touches and the frayed edges alike. We never publish prices, we never sell a kind word, and every review carries the name of the person who slept in the bed.

When a link here leads you to book a room, the property may pay us a small commission. That keeps the collection free to read — and it never earns a hotel a better score.

Our method

How a hotel earns its place in the edit

A consistent, four-step process sits behind every review you read.

01

We book & pay

Rooms are reserved the way any guest would — no comps, no "media rate", and no advance warning to the hotel.

02

We stay the night

Our writer lives in the room for at least one full night, testing the bed, the bathroom, the quiet, the Wi-Fi and the service.

03

We listen widely

We read hundreds of recent guest reviews so a single wonderful (or dreadful) night never skews the picture.

04

We write it plainly

A signed, first-person review with a clear verdict — praise where it's earned, and candour where it isn't.
